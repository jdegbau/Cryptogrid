      How to play

      1. Fill every empty cell with a number from 1 to 9. Numbers can repeat.
      2. Each row and column must match its clue. Rows read left to right, columns top to bottom.
      3. Submit when the grid is full. You have five guesses, and every puzzle has exactly one solution.

      After each guess

      • ✓Correct. The cell locks in.
      • ≈Close. Within 2 of the answer.
      • ✗Wrong. More than 2 away.
      • 4Given. Pre-filled for you.

      The number pad shows what you've already tried in the selected cell. A new puzzle arrives every midnight (Chicago time), getting harder from Monday to Sunday.
